How to make a resume in 2025

75% of resumes are read by computers.

Keyword matching

Tailor your resume to each job posting. Use the exact same keywords, skills, and tools as the job posting.

Make your resume readable by computers

Don't use images, multiple columns, etc. Scanning systems will not be able to read your resume correctly.
